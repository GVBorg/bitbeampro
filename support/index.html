Support

For help with BitBeamPro, email bbeamsupport@gmail.com.

Reporting an issue

Include your app version, macOS version, lighting protocol and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, API keys or private project files.

Connection troubleshooting

Check that your lighting equipment is reachable on the local network and that the protocol and universe settings match.
